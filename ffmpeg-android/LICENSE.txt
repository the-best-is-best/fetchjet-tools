ffmpeg-android for FetchJet

libffmpeg.so and libffprobe.so are the ffmpeg and ffprobe programs of FFmpeg 7.1.1
(https://ffmpeg.org), built with --enable-version3: LGPL version 3 or later.
They contain:
- LAME 3.100 (LGPL 2.0 or later), https://lame.sourceforge.io
- Opus 1.5.2 (BSD 3-clause), https://opus-codec.org
- Mbed TLS 3.6.8 (Apache 2.0), https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls
- zlib from the Android NDK (zlib licence)

The recipe that builds them is ffmpeg-android/build.sh in https://github.com/the-best-is-best/fetchjet-tools.
